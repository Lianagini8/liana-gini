Production questions
How do I know who’s actually making my order?

We match the factory to the product, construction and volume, work on site and know who the order goes to.

Will the order match the sample?

The reference sample is the standard for the whole order. If the factory is honest and the sample is approved, the order will match it.

The real risk is elsewhere: a dishonest factory may swap the fabric or composition in the sample itself. They promise one cotton and use a cheaper one. I check this personally before the sample goes into production.

How long does the first collection take?

Developing a collection from scratch usually takes 3–6 months. It’s a cycle: development, samples, fabrics, approvals, production and quality control.
